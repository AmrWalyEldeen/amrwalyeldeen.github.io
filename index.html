About Me

My academic work integrates experimental cell biology with proteomics and bioinformatics to discover clinically useful biomarkers. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. on proteomic profiling of circulating exosomes from breast cancer patients, with the goal of identifying exosomal signatures that can support practical, cost-effective, and non-invasive liquid-biopsy approaches for early detection and disease monitoring.
